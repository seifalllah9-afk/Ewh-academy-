Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes, all our classes are conducted online via Zoom or similar platforms. This allows you to learn from anywhere in Egypt or abroad, with flexible scheduling that fits your lifestyle.

Do you offer free trial sessions?

Absolutely! We offer a free 20-minute trial session where you can meet the trainer, discuss your goals, and experience our teaching methodology before committing.

Can beginners join your courses?

Yes, we welcome students of all levels from complete beginners to advanced learners. Our placement test ensures you're placed in the appropriate level class.

Are classes individual or group?

We offer both options. Group classes have 6 to 8 students for interactive learning, while one-to-one classes offer personalized attention for faster progress.

How do placement tests work?

Our placement test evaluates your grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, and speaking skills. It takes about 30 minutes and can be completed online before your trial session or through a phone call.

What are the course fees?

Fees vary depending on the course type and duration. Group classes fees differ from one to one and the fees varies according to the course type that you have chosen , Contact us for detailed pricing.
